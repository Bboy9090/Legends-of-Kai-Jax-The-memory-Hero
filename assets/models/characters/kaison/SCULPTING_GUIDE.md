# Kaison Sculpting Guide
## Step-by-Step Beast-Kin Sculpting Workflow

**Character:** Kaison - The Swift Guardian 🦊  
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ Intermediate  
**Estimated Time:** 10-18 hours

---

## 🚀 Phase 1: Blockout Refinement (30 min - 1 hour)

### Step 1.1: Verify Blockout
- [ ] Body: 0.85 × 0.55 × 0.95 units
- [ ] 2 energy tails visible
- [ ] Head with elongated snout
- [ ] Proportions feel right

### Step 1.2: Refine Body Shape
1. **Enter Sculpt Mode** (Tab)
2. **Use Grab Tool** (G)
3. **Reshape:**
   - Lean, agile form
   - Tactical stance
   - Guardian posture
   - Smooth curves

---

## 🎨 Phase 2: Primary Forms (3-5 hours)

### Step 2.1: Body Sculpting

**Create Agile Form:**
1. **Streamlined Body:**
   - Lean, athletic build
   - Tactical posture
   - Guardian stance
   - Smooth surfaces

2. **Subtle Muscle Definition:**
   - Arms: Tactical strength
   - Legs: Agile, springy
   - Torso: Lean, defined
   - **Keep it subtle**

### Step 2.2: Head Sculpting (CRITICAL)

**Fox Snout is Key:**
1. **Elongate Snout:**
   - Use Grab to extend forward
   - Create fox-like muzzle
   - Rounded tip
   - Distinct from hedgehog

2. **Facial Features:**
   - **Eyes:** Intelligent, protective
   - **Mouth:** Subtle line
   - **Ears:** Pointed, alert
   - **Expression:** Guardian awareness

3. **Fox Characteristics:**
   - Distinctive snout
   - Alert expression
   - Intelligent eyes
   - Guardian stance

### Step 2.3: Limb Sculpting

**Tactical Limbs:**
1. **Arms:**
   - Agile, tactical
   - Ready to protect
   - Smooth flow

2. **Legs:**
   - Springy, agile
   - Ready to move
   - Guardian stance

---

## 🦊 Phase 3: Twin Tail System (3-5 hours)

### Step 3.1: Shape Energy Tails

**Each of 2 Tails:**
1. **Tail Base:**
   - Thicker at base
   - Attached to body
   - Good angle

2. **Tail Body:**
   - Taper smoothly
   - Energy-like flow
   - 1.2 units length

3. **Tail Tip:**
   - Sharp point
   - Energy emitter
   - Ready for effects

### Step 3.2: Tail Positioning

**Twin Tail Layout:**
- **Left Tail:** Angled left, upward
- **Right Tail:** Angled right, upward
- **Balance:** Both maintain balance
- **Flow:** Energy-like movement

### Step 3.3: Tail Details

**Energy Form:**
- Semi-transparent appearance (materials)
- Smooth, flowing shape
- Energy particle ready
- Guardian energy aesthetic

---

## 🎨 Phase 4: Surface Details (2-4 hours)

### Step 4.1: Fur Direction

**Fox Fur Flow:**
1. **Direction:**
   - Back to front
   - Sleek, aerodynamic
   - Guardian aesthetic

2. **Texture:**
   - Subtle fur lines
   - Smooth surface
   - Golden-orange ready

### Step 4.2: Guardian Details

**Tactical Features:**
1. **Posture:**
   - Alert stance
   - Ready to protect
   - Guardian awareness

2. **Expression:**
   - Intelligent eyes
   - Protective stance
   - Tactical focus

---

## ✨ Phase 5: Polish (2-4 hours)

### Step 5.1: Final Refinement

**Smooth Everything:**
1. **Complete Pass:**
   - Smooth entire model
   - Refine all details
   - Check proportions

2. **Quality Check:**
   - Fox snout distinct
   - Twin tails balanced
   - Guardian posture clear
   - Ready for retopology

---

## 🎯 Key Focus Areas

### Must Get Right:
1. **Fox Snout** - Distinctive, elongated
2. **Twin Tails** - Balanced, energy-like
3. **Guardian Posture** - Alert, protective
4. **Golden-Orange** - Color in materials

### Don't Overdo:
1. **Fur Detail** - Stylized, not realistic
2. **Muscle Definition** - Keep subtle
3. **Tail Complexity** - Simple, flowing

## 🚨 Common Issues

### Issue: Snout not fox-like enough
**Fix:** Elongate more, make it more distinct from hedgehog

### Issue: Tails unbalanced
**Fix:** Check positioning, ensure both are visible

### Issue: Not guardian-like
**Fix:** Adjust posture, make more alert/protective
