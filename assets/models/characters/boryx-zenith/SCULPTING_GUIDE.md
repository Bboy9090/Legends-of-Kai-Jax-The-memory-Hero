## 🚀 Phase 1: Blockout Refinement (1-2 hours)

## 🎯 Key Focus Areas

### Must Get Right:
1. **Massive Form** - Heavyweight, powerful
2. **Source Star** - Central, prominent
3. **Mixed Texture** - 50/50 fur and scales
4. **Guardian Stance** - Powerful, protective

1. **Details** - Keep it clean, powerful
2. **Complexity** - Focus on form
3. **Realism** - Stylized, powerful
