# Boryx Zenith Retopology Guide
## Clean Topology for Massive Dragon-Bear

**Character:** Boryx Zenith - Guardian King 🐻  
**Estimated Time:** 5-9 hours  
**Target Polycount:** 45,000-55,000 tris (LOD0)

---

## 🚀 Phase 1: Setup (30 min)

### Step 1.1: Prepare High-Poly
1. **Duplicate Sculpt** → Hide original
2. **Create Base Mesh** → UV Sphere (massive scale)
3. **Add Shrinkwrap** → Target high-poly

---

## 🔧 Phase 2: Body Topology (3-5 hours)

### Step 2.1: Build Massive Topology

**Edge Loops:**
- Horizontal: Chest, Waist, Hips (wider spacing)
- Vertical: Front, Sides, Back
- **Key:** Heavyweight frame, fewer loops needed

### Step 2.2: Mixed Texture Areas

**Fur Areas:**
- Smooth topology
- Natural flow
- 50% coverage

**Scale Areas:**
- Sharper topology
- Defined edges
- 50% coverage

---

## ⚡ Phase 3: Source Star (30 min)

### Step 3.1: Source Star Topology

**Chest-Mounted Star:**
1. **Create Star Base**
   - Ico sphere
   - Low polycount
   - Embedded in chest

2. **Refine Shape**
   - Use Shrinkwrap
   - Match high-poly
   - Amber energy ready

---

## 🎨 Phase 4: UV Unwrapping (2-4 hours)

### Step 4.1: Mark Seams
- Under arms
- Inner legs
- Back center
- Around Source Star

### Step 4.2: Unwrap & Pack
- Unwrap (U)
- Pack Islands
- 80-90% usage

---

- [ ] UVs unwrapped
