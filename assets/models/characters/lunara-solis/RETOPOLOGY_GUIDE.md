# Lunara Solis Retopology Guide
## Clean Topology for 9 Tails

**Character:** Lunara Solis - The Harmony Anchor 🌙  
**Estimated Time:** 6-10 hours  
**Target Polycount:** 50,000-60,000 tris (LOD0)

---

## 🚀 Phase 1: Setup (30 min)

### Step 1.1: Prepare High-Poly
1. **Duplicate Sculpt** → Hide original
2. **Create Base Mesh** → UV Sphere (tall, elegant)
3. **Add Shrinkwrap** → Target high-poly

---

## 🔧 Phase 2: Body Topology (3-5 hours)

### Step 2.1: Build Elegant Topology

**Edge Loops:**
- Horizontal: Chest, Waist, Hips
- Vertical: Front, Sides, Back
- **Key:** Maintain elegant, tall form

### Step 2.2: Digitigrade Legs

**Animal-Like Legs:**
- Extra loops at joints
- Digitigrade stance ready
- Smooth deformation

---

## 🌙 Phase 3: Nine-Tail System (4-6 hours)

### Step 3.1: Gold Solar Tails (5 Tails)

**Each of 5 Gold Tails:**
1. **Create Tail Base**
   - Start with cylinder
   - 10-12 sides
   - Match tail position

2. **Refine Tail Shape**
   - Use Shrinkwrap
   - Match high-poly
   - Fire particle ready

3. **Tail Edge Loops**
   - Base loop
   - Mid loops (4 flex points)
   - Tip loop
   - **Total:** 12 bones per tail (60 bones total)

### Step 3.2: Silver Lunar Tails (4 Tails)

**Each of 4 Silver Tails:**
1. **Same process as gold tails**
2. **12 bones per tail** (48 bones total)
3. **Shimmer effects ready**

### Step 3.3: Tail Optimization

**Optimize for Performance:**
- Reduce polycount if needed
- Maintain flow
- Animation-ready

---

## 🎨 Phase 4: UV Unwrapping (3-5 hours)

### Step 4.1: Mark Seams
- Under arms
- Inner legs
- Back center
- Around each tail base (9 tails!)

### Step 4.2: Unwrap & Pack
- Unwrap (U)
- Pack Islands (may need multiple UV sets)
- 80-90% usage

---

## ✅ Quality Check

- [ ] Polycount: 50,000-60,000 tris
- [ ] All quads
- [ ] 9 tails: 12 bones each (108 bones total)
- [ ] UVs unwrapped
- [ ] Ready for texturing
