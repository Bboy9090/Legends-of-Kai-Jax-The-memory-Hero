# Umbra-Flux Retopology Guide
## Clean Topology for 5 Elemental Tails

**Character:** Umbra-Flux - Velocity Wraith 🐺  
**Estimated Time:** 5-9 hours  
**Target Polycount:** 40,000-50,000 tris (LOD0)

---

## 🚀 Phase 1: Setup (30 min)

### Step 1.1: Prepare High-Poly
1. **Duplicate Sculpt** → Hide original
2. **Create Base Mesh** → UV Sphere (horizontal stance)
3. **Add Shrinkwrap** → Target high-poly

---

## 🔧 Phase 2: Body Topology (3-5 hours)

### Step 2.1: Build Streamlined Topology

**Edge Loops:**
- Horizontal: Chest, Waist, Hips
- Vertical: Front, Sides, Back
- **Key:** Maintain horizontal, streamlined form

### Step 2.2: Quadrupedal Limbs

**Four Legs:**
- Front legs: Clean topology
- Back legs: Clean topology
- **Key:** Quadrupedal stance ready

---

## ⚡ Phase 3: Five Elemental Tails (3-5 hours)

### Step 3.1: Tail Topology

**Each of 5 Tails:**
1. **Create Tail Base**
   - Start with cylinder
   - 10-12 sides
   - Match tail position

2. **Refine Tail Shape**
   - Use Shrinkwrap
   - Match high-poly
   - Elemental energy ready

3. **Tail Edge Loops**
   - Base loop
   - Mid loops (4 flex points)
   - Tip loop
   - **Total:** 10 bones per tail (50 bones total)

### Step 3.2: Quill-Blade Topology

**5 Quill-Blades:**
- 8-12 sides each
- Clean edge loops
- Crystalline ready

---

## 🎨 Phase 4: UV Unwrapping (2-4 hours)

### Step 4.1: Mark Seams
- Under front legs
- Under back legs
- Back center
- Around tail bases (5 tails)
- Around quill-blades

### Step 4.2: Unwrap & Pack
- Unwrap (U)
- Pack Islands
- 80-90% usage

---

## ✅ Quality Check

- [ ] Polycount: 40,000-50,000 tris
- [ ] All quads
- [ ] 5 tails: 10 bones each
- [ ] UVs unwrapped
- [ ] Ready for texturing
