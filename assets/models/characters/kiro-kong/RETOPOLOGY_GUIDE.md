# Kiro Kong Retopology Guide
## Clean Topology for Stone Armor

**Character:** Kiro Kong - Primal Breaker 🦍  
**Estimated Time:** 5-9 hours  
**Target Polycount:** 45,000-55,000 tris (LOD0)

---

## 🚀 Phase 1: Setup (30 min)

### Step 1.1: Prepare High-Poly
1. **Duplicate Sculpt** → Hide original
2. **Create Base Mesh** → UV Sphere (massive, hunched)
3. **Add Shrinkwrap** → Target high-poly

---

## 🔧 Phase 2: Body Topology (3-5 hours)

### Step 2.1: Build Massive Topology

**Edge Loops:**
- Horizontal: Chest, Waist, Hips (wider spacing)
- Vertical: Front, Sides, Back
- **Key:** Heavyweight frame, fewer loops

### Step 2.2: Massive Limbs

**Tree Trunk Limbs:**
- Arms: 4.0 units (massive)
- Legs: 3.0 units (tree trunks)
- **Key:** Primal strength ready

---

## ⚡ Phase 3: Stone Armor (1-2 hours)

### Step 3.1: Armor Topology

**Stone Plates:**
1. **Chest Plate**
   - Separate mesh or integrated
   - Clean topology
   - Stone texture ready

2. **Shoulder Plates**
   - Separate meshes
   - Clean topology
   - Weathered ready

### Step 3.2: Armor Details

**Damage States:**
- Pristine topology
- Cracked topology (optional)
- Shattered topology (optional)

---

## 🎨 Phase 4: UV Unwrapping (2-4 hours)

### Step 4.1: Mark Seams
- Under arms
- Inner legs
- Back center
- Around armor plates

### Step 4.2: Unwrap & Pack
- Unwrap (U)
- Pack Islands
- 80-90% usage

---

## ✅ Quality Check

- [ ] Polycount: 45,000-55,000 tris
- [ ] All quads
- [ ] Stone armor topology clean
- [ ] UVs unwrapped
- [ ] Ready for texturing

---

**Ready?** Start retopology! 🦍
