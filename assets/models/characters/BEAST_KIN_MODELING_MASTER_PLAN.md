### Visual Standards (ALL Characters)
- ✅ **Jagged Silhouettes** - No smooth/clean Nintendo aesthetics
- ✅ **Heavyweight Frames** - Bronx-grit aesthetic
- ✅ **PBR Materials** - Physically-based rendering
- ✅ **Distinct DNA Profiles** - No direct 1:1 mappings
- ✅ **Bronx Grit Filter** - 0.08 opacity asphalt overlay
