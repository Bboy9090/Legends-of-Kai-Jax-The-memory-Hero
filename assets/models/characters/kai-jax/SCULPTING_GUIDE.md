# Kai-Jax Sculpting Guide
## Step-by-Step Beast-Kin Sculpting Workflow

**Character:** Kai-Jax - The Memory Hero ⚡🦊🦔  
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced  
**Estimated Time:** 12-20 hours

---

## 🚀 Phase 1: Blockout Refinement (1-2 hours)

### Step 1.1: Verify Blockout
- [ ] Spherical body: 1.0 × 1.0 × 1.07 units
- [ ] 3 memory tails (Gold/Blue/White)
- [ ] 6 quills visible
- [ ] Proportions feel right

### Step 1.2: Refine Spherical Body
1. **Enter Sculpt Mode** (Tab)
2. **Maintain Spherical Form:**
   - Keep round shape (Kirby-esque)
   - Smooth all surfaces
   - Compact, fusion form

---

## 🎨 Phase 2: Primary Forms (4-6 hours)

### Step 2.1: Body Sculpting (Star-Slime Chimera)

**Create Fusion Form:**
1. **Spherical Base:**
   - Maintain round shape
   - Smooth all surfaces
   - Compact, fusion aesthetic

2. **Internal Nebula Effect:**
   - Will be in materials (volume shader)
   - Sculpt smooth surface for nebula
   - Prepare for volume effect

3. **Fusion Characteristics:**
   - Compact, spherical
   - Smooth surfaces
   - Fusion aesthetic

### Step 2.2: Head Sculpting

**Integrated Head:**
1. **Head Shape:**
   - Part of spherical body
   - Not separate
   - Smooth integration

2. **Facial Features:**
   - **Eyes:** Neon-gold, slit pupils
   - **Expression:** Wise, burdened, hopeful
   - **Fusion look:** Hedgehog + Fox

### Step 2.3: Limb Sculpting

**Compact Limbs:**
1. **Arms:**
   - Short, compact
   - Smooth integration
   - Fusion form

2. **Legs:**
   - Short, compact
   - Smooth integration
   - Fusion form

---

## ⚡ Phase 3: Three Memory Tails (4-6 hours)

### Step 3.1: Tail 1 - Gold (Velocity/Jaxon)

**Gold Tail Sculpting:**
1. **Shape:**
   - Liquid ink appearance
   - Flowing, organic
   - 1.5 units length
   - Taper smoothly

2. **Details:**
   - Smooth, flowing
   - Energy-ready
   - Speed-focused

### Step 3.2: Tail 2 - Blue (Shielding/Kaison)

**Blue Tail Sculpting:**
1. **Shape:**
   - Thicker than gold tail
   - Semi-solid appearance
   - 1.5 units length
   - Defensive form

2. **Details:**
   - Protective shape
   - Guardian energy
   - Web-tether ready

### Step 3.3: Tail 3 - White (Harmony/Hope)

**White Tail Sculpting:**
1. **Shape:**
   - Translucent appearance
   - Ghostly, flowing
   - 1.5 units length
   - Harmony form

2. **Details:**
   - Most ethereal
   - Memory echo ready
   - Hope-focused

### Step 3.4: Tail Positioning

**Three-Tail Layout:**
- **Gold Tail:** Left side, angled
- **Blue Tail:** Center, straight
- **White Tail:** Right side, angled
- **Balance:** All three visible
- **Flow:** Memory system aesthetic

---

## 🎨 Phase 4: Quills & Details (2-4 hours)

### Step 4.1: Quill Sculpting

**6 Quills (Hedgehog Heritage):**
1. **Shape Each Quill:**
   - Sharp tips
   - Electric blue ready
   - Jagged, angular
   - Hedgehog heritage

2. **Positioning:**
   - Back of body
   - Angled outward
   - Speed-focused

### Step 4.2: Surface Details

**Fusion Surface:**
1. **Smooth Surfaces:**
   - All surfaces smooth
   - Prepare for nebula
   - Fusion aesthetic

2. **Integration:**
   - All parts integrated
   - Smooth transitions
   - Fusion complete

---

## ✨ Phase 5: Polish (2-4 hours)

### Step 5.1: Final Refinement

**Complete Model:**
1. **Smooth Everything:**
   - Entire model smooth
   - All transitions clean
   - Fusion form clear

2. **Quality Check:**
   - Spherical body maintained
   - 3 tails distinct
   - 6 quills positioned
   - Ready for retopology

---

## 🎯 Key Focus Areas

### Must Get Right:
1. **Spherical Form** - Maintain round shape
2. **3 Memory Tails** - All distinct and positioned
3. **Fusion Aesthetic** - Hedgehog + Fox combined
4. **Internal Nebula** - Smooth surface for volume

### Don't Overdo:
1. **Details** - Keep it simple, fusion-focused
2. **Complexity** - Smooth, clean surfaces
3. **Realism** - Stylized, not realistic

---

## ✅ Success Criteria

**Kai-Jax is sculpted correctly when:**
- ✅ Spherical body maintained
- ✅ 3 memory tails distinct
- ✅ 6 quills positioned
- ✅ Fusion aesthetic clear
- ✅ Ready for retopology

---

## 🚨 Common Issues

### Issue: Body not spherical enough
**Fix:** Use Smooth brush, maintain round shape

### Issue: Tails not distinct
**Fix:** Make each tail unique shape, different thickness

### Issue: Not fusion-like
**Fix:** Ensure hedgehog + fox elements visible
