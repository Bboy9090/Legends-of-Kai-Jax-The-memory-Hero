### 1. Confirmed Playable Heroes
1. **KAI — Memory-Web Heir**
   - **Silhouette & Palette**: Ashen silver-gray fur, sabertooth fangs, violet eyes & memory markings, violet + ember-orange energy, dark urban combat clothing, four articulated spider-like mechanical/bioenergetic limbs.
   - **Specialization**: High mobility, web-based traversal, crowd control, venom attacks, stealth, memory manipulation, rescue & environmental puzzle solving.
   - **Canonical Abilities**: `Memory Weave`, `Venom Strike`, `Web of Pyraxis`, `Shadow Ink`, `Spider-Limb Mobility`.

2. **JAX — Storm Fang**
   - **Silhouette & Palette**: Dark charcoal fur, electric-blue eyes, powerful sabertooth features, flowing storm-charged mane, black + blue combat equipment, electric-blue energy.
   - **Specialization**: Speed, storm pressure, lightning attacks, heavy impact combat, rapid displacement, environmental disruption.
   - **Canonical Abilities**: `Storm Pressure`, `Lightning Channeling`, `Displacement Burst`, `Thryxen Senses`, `Storm Mane`.

3. **KAI-JAX — The Memory Hero (Canon-Gated Fusion)**
   - **Silhouette & Palette**: Unified sabertooth appearance, hybrid armor & markings, articulated spider limbs, violet + ember-orange + electric-blue energy.
   - **Critical Tail Rule**: Begins with strictly **three primary physical tails** representing **Ember**, **Storm**, and **Memory**. Later nine-domain progression manifests as unlockable **Energy Domains** / temporary aura manifestations—never adding permanent extra physical tails unless explicitly revised by an authoritative story bible.
   - **Story Gate**: Locked until the climax of *Mission 1: The City Bites Back* (Phase 2 of the Fang Enforcer confrontation), carrying narrative weight and balanced energy/cooldown rules.

### 2. Heritage & Mythology (The Four Powers)
- **Myrr'Kai (Memory)**: Memory reconstruction, hidden history, perception, and identity.
- **Pyraxis (Sacrifice & Ember)**: Fire, venom, sacrifice, and the cost of power.
- **Kar-Voth (Hunger & Storm)**: Storm pressure, overwhelming force, survival, and destructive potential.
- **Thryxen (Law & Sovereignty)**: Predatory awareness, discipline, control, and authority.

### 3. Family & Mentors
- **Bovar**: Adoptive father of Kai and Jax; grounds their survival in Ashblock Heights.
- **Boryn**: Sabertooth tiger, protector, and mentor.
- **Borax**: Sabertooth lion, associated with law and discipline (later tied to *Borax Zenith*).

### 4. World Framework — The Raging City (Eight Districts)
1. **Ashblock Heights** *(Playable Vertical Slice)* — Rooftop tenements, wet alleys, Fang Syndicate blockade, Alley Gate, Disrupted Memory Site.
2. **Sector-7** *(Campaign Expansion)* — Industrial transit corridors and heavy security chokepoints.
3. **Neon Ward** *(Campaign Expansion)* — Elevated high-density commerce and Neon Prince territory.
4. **Iron Market** *(Campaign Expansion)* — Smuggling hub and Blood Broker stronghold.
5. **Undercrown** *(Campaign Expansion)* — Subterranean tunnels and Undercrown Sentinel domain.
6. **Zenith Spires** *(Campaign Expansion)* — High-altitude towers of Law and Borax Zenith.
7. **Erasure Fields** *(Campaign Expansion)* — Contested anomaly zone of the Erasure Choir.
8. **Memory Nexus** *(Campaign Expansion)* — Heart of Myrr'Kai convergence and the Hollow Architect.
