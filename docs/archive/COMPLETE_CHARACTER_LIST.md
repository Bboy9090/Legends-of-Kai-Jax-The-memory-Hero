## ⚡🦊🦔 KAI-JAX - THE MEMORY HERO

### **Beast Hybrid:** Star-Slime Chimera
**Species:** Hedgehog-Kitsune-Lupine Fusion

### **What He Looks Like:**
- **Body:** Compact, spherical (Kirby-esque), 3.5-4.0 feet tall
- **Fur:** Obsidian charcoal (#1A1A1A) with internal purple-cyan nebulae visible through semi-transparent skin
- **Three Memory Tails:**
  - **Gold Tail** (Jax Strand): Speed and courage, liquid ink with hard-light echoes
  - **Blue Tail** (Kai Strand): Strength and guardianship, ink smoke barriers
  - **White Tail** (Father's Strand): Harmony and hope, translucent ghostly anchor
- **Eyes:** Neon-gold (#FFD700) sage-mode slit pupils, glowing with wisdom
- **Quills:** 24 long electric blue quills (300% extended), crackling with energy
- **Aura:** Iridescent memory aura shifting between blue, gold, white, and magenta
- **Jacket:** High-tech white tactical Star-Force flight jacket with golden Badge of Sovereignty
- **Memory Orbs:** 5 floating blue orbs orbiting around him

**Overall:** A compact, powerful fusion creature with three distinct memory tails, glowing gold eyes, electric quills, and a cosmic aura. The perfect fusion of hedgehog speed, kitsune cunning, and lupine strength.
