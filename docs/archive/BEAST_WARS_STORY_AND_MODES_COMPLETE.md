## 🎮 ADDITIONAL GAME MODES

### 🗼 TOWERS TOURNAMENT (Mortal Kombat Style)
- **Bronze Tower**: 10 floors, Difficulty 1
- **Silver Tower**: 15 floors, Difficulty 3 (unlock after Bronze)
- **Gold Tower**: 20 floors, Difficulty 5 (unlock after Silver)
- **Platinum Tower**: 25 floors, Difficulty 7 (unlock after Gold)
- **Diamond Tower**: 30 floors, Difficulty 9 (unlock after Platinum)
- **Legendary Tower**: 50 floors, Difficulty 10 (unlock after Diamond)

### ⚔️ VERSUS MODES
- **1v1 Versus**: One-on-one. Best of 3 rounds.
- **2v2 Team Battle**: Tag team mechanics. Two fighters per side.
- **3v3 Squad Battle**: Ultimate team synergy. Three fighters per side.

### 🔥 GAUNTLET MODE
- **Endless Waves**: Survive as long as you can.
- **Increasing Difficulty**: Each wave harder than the last.
- **Difficulty Levels**: Easy (10-20 waves), Medium (20-40), Hard (40-60), Extreme (60+)
- **Team Support**: Up to 4 players

### 💀 SURVIVOR MODE
- **Last Beast Standing**: Battle royale style.
- **One Life**: Eliminated when health reaches 0.
- **Player Counts**: 4, 6, or 8 players
- **Winner Takes All**: XP, currency, exclusive loot
