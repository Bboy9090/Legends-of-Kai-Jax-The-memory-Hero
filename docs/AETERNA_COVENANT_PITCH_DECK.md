## SLIDE 11: RISK MITIGATION

**[VISUAL: Risk analysis matrix]**

### ADDRESSING CONCERNS HEAD-ON

**Risk 1: "Platform fighters are niche"**
- **Mitigation:** Narrative-first approach expands audience (RPG players)
- **Evidence:** Smash Ultimate (32M) proves market exists

**Risk 2: "9-book saga is ambitious"**
- **Mitigation:** Book 1 launches complete, subsequent books = DLC
- **Evidence:** Telltale Games model (episodic narrative success)

**Risk 3: "Competing with Smash"**
- **Mitigation:** Different audience (mature, narrative-driven)
- **Evidence:** Street Fighter vs. Mortal Kombat (both succeed in same genre)

**Risk 4: "Original IP is untested"**
- **Mitigation:** Matte-mythic aesthetic + proven mechanics = familiarity + novelty
- **Evidence:** Hollow Knight (original IP, 3M+ units)
