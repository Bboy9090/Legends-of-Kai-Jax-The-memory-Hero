# THE AETERNA COVENANT: CINEMATIC TRAILER SCRIPT
## "The Silent Year" | Book 1 Launch Trailer

**Length:** 2:30 (90 seconds for TV spot)  
**Tone:** Matte-Mythic, Heavy, Inevitable  
**Visual Style:** Dune meets The Batman trailer energy  
**Music:** Cinematic orchestral with electronic bass (dynamic intensity)

## TRAILER STRUCTURE

### [00:00-00:15] PROLOGUE — THE SILENCE

**[FADE IN: BLACK SCREEN]**

*Total silence. No music. Hold for 2 seconds.*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR THE IRON-WILL)**  
*(Deep, resonant, gravelly. Like tectonic plates shifting.)*

"They call it the Silence. The day the noise of the world just... stopped."

**[CUT TO]**

**EXT. THE PRIME PILLAR — RUINS — DAY (GREYSCALE)**

*Slow drone shot over the shattered remains of the ancient arena. The sky is a bruised purple and grey. Dust hangs heavy. It looks like a graveyard of giants. The camera moves slowly, revealing broken obsidian pillars and scorched ground.*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR)**

"The legends you knew? The ones who jumped high and ran fast?"

*The camera settles on a massive crater in the center of the arena.*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR)**

"The Void ate them first."

### [00:15-00:45] ACT I — THE SURVIVORS

**[CUT TO]**

**INT. IRON CANYON BUNKER — NIGHT**

*A flickering orange light. We see silhouettes of the Resistance. A massive TECH-APE (TRX-01, Tails-analog) solder-welding a piece of scrap armor. Sparks fly. Close-up on weathered hands.*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR)**

"Now all we got left is the grit."

**[CUT TO]**

**INT. IRON CANYON BUNKER — CLOSE-UP**

*A scarred RED-LUPINE BRAWLER (REX-BLOCK, Knuckles-analog) wrapping his fists in torn cloth. He stares at a faded mural of the old heroes painted on the wall. His eyes reflect the firelight. Defiant. Stubborn.*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR)**

"The ones too stubborn to fade away."

**[CUT TO]**

**EXT. THE WHISPERING WILDS — TWILIGHT**

*A massive shadow passes over the treetops. The camera tilts up to reveal MALAKOR THE SILENCER standing on a cliff edge. He is a glitch in reality—a jagged, flickering silhouette against the dying light. The sound design here is a horrible, low-frequency static hum that grows louder.*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR)**

"The Source thinks it won. It thinks because the pillars fell, the fight is over."

*Malakor's form flickers. Static tears across the screen. The world turns greyscale around him.*

**[CUT TO BLACK]**

### [00:45-01:15] ACT II — THE ANCHOR

**[HEARTBEAT STARTS]**

*Slow. Heavy. THUMP-THUMP. Low-frequency bass.*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR)**

**[CUT TO]**

**EXT. THE VOID-RIFT — UNKNOWN LOCATION**

*A swirling vortex of absolute blackness. The camera slowly pushes in. The static hum intensifies.*

**THUMP-THUMP.** *(Louder)*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR)**

"...You don't need light to fight in the dark."

*Heavy, cinematic drums and synthetic horns. The intensity builds. Strings enter. The beat accelerates.*

### [01:15-02:00] ACT III — THE REVEAL

**[FULL REVEAL — KAI-JAX]**

*Kai-Jax lands in a three-point stance, cracking the ground beneath him. The matte-black armor fused to his Lupine/Vulpine muscle glints in the firelight. The THREE TAILS ignite behind him—burning Amber, Blue, and Red energy. They lash out like living whips.*

*Close-up on Kai-Jax's face. Heterochromatic eyes (amber/blue) lock onto the camera. Determined. Unified.*

**VOICE OVER (KAI-JAX)**  
*(A dual-layered voice—Jaxon's rage and Kaison's calm fused together.)*

"We remember."

**SHOT 3:** Kai-Jax unleashes a three-tail spiral attack that clears the screen. The Amber tail (Bovarr's Anchor) intercepts a Void-Scythe mid-swing.

**SHOT 4:** Malakor recoils, static tearing across his form. His glitch intensifies.

**SHOT 5:** Kai-Jax charges forward, the three tails spinning into a blinding pillar of light.

**VOICE OVER (KAI-JAX)**

"My father didn't leave us..."

**[CUT TO]**

**INT. PRIME PILLAR — MEMORY FLASHBACK**

*Quick flash: Bovarr standing between Malakor and two small silhouettes (Jaxon & Kaison). The Star-Forge ignites behind them. Bovarr turns, blood on his teeth, a defiant smile.*

**VOICE OVER (BOVARR)**

"Stand up, sons. You are the Covenant."

**[CUT TO]**

**EXT. PRIME PILLAR — PRESENT**

*Kai-Jax's three tails spin into the Iron-Will Overdrive. Bovarr's spectral form manifests behind him—a massive Ursine silhouette made of amber light.*

**VOICE OVER (KAI-JAX)**

"...He just changed where he was standing."

**[THE MUSIC PEAKS]**

*Thunderous bass drop. The three tails explode into a screen-clearing attack. Malakor is thrown backward, static consuming him.*

## VOICE ACTING NOTES

### BOVARR THE IRON-WILL
- **Casting:** Deep, resonant baritone (think Idris Elba or Keith David)
- **Delivery:** Calm but heavy. Every word feels like a promise.
- **Audio Processing:** Slight reverb/echo (spectral effect)

### KAI-JAX
- **Casting:** Dual-layer voice (one actor for Jaxon, one for Kaison, mixed)
- **Delivery:** Synchronized. Power and precision in harmony.
- **Audio Processing:** Subtle layering (two voices, one body)

---

## VISUAL EFFECTS NOTES

### MALAKOR'S STATIC
- **Reference:** Screen-space distortion shader
- **Effect:** Geometric tearing, flickering, greyscale corruption
- **Sound:** Low-frequency static hum (growing intensity)

### KAI-JAX'S THREE TAILS
- **Reference:** Dynamic cloth simulation with emissive particles
- **Effect:** Living energy whips, color-coded (Red/Blue/Amber)
- **Sound:** Energy crackling, bass thrum

### BOVARR'S SPECTRAL FORM
- **Reference:** Volumetric lighting, amber glow
- **Effect:** Transparent Ursine silhouette, heat distortion
- **Sound:** Deep bass rumble, heartbeat synchronization
