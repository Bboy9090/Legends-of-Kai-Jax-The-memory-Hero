A narrative-first platform fighter where mythic Beastfolk heroes fight a cosmic war between Noise (Life) and Silence (The Void). Built on Smash-style mechanics but elevated by a mature, cinematic 9-book saga. We aren't cloning Smash; we're out-mything it.

## 🎮 GAMEPLAY CORE SYSTEMS

### 1. THE RESONANCE SYSTEM (Combat Flow)

**Resonance Tiers:**
- **Dormant (0-49%):** Basic moves, minimal visual glow
- **Harmonized (50-99%):** Core ignites, attacks gain elemental "Memory" trails
- **Ascended (100%):** Apex form, unlock "Aeterna Break" (Final Smash equivalent)

**How Resonance Builds:**
- Aggressive play (hits landed)
- Precision (perfect shields, counter-attacks)
- Emotional connection (combo synergy, team coordination)
- Time (passive slow build)

**Visual Feedback:**
- Core glow intensity scales with resonance
- At 100%, character emits "Heat Distortion" effect
- Screen effects: color saturation increases, particle density spikes

**Resonance Loss:**
- Taking damage reduces resonance
- Missing attacks reduces resonance
- Being knocked off stage reduces resonance significantly

---

### 2. THE CORE SYSTEM (Replaces Percentage Damage)

**Core Stability:**
- Starts at 100% stability
- Taking hits reduces stability
- Low stability (<30%) = screen glitching, control lag
- Stability at 0% = automatic ring-out (forced respawn)

**Stability Protection:**
- High resonance = damage reduction
- Perfect shields = zero stability loss
- Counter-attacks = stability regained

**Visual Representation:**
- Core icon in HUD corner
- Color-coded: Green (>70%), Yellow (30-70%), Red (<30%)
- Glitch intensity increases as stability decreases

---

### 3. THE PATERNAL ANCHOR (Kai-Jax Exclusive Mechanic)

**Bovarr's Voice System:**
- At 100% Resonance: Voice replaces background music
- Provides tactical advice ("Watch left!", "Counter now!")
- Prevents "Rejection" debuff (inability to act under pressure)

**The 3rd Tail (Defensive Shield):**
- Automatically blocks 25% of projectile damage
- Activates at critical health or max resonance
- Visual: Amber energy lash that intercepts attacks
- Cooldown: 30 seconds after activation

**Internal Navigator:**
- Jaxon & Kaison argue constantly in mind
- Bovarr's voice provides guidance to synchronize
- Player must balance "Grit" (Jaxon) and "Wisdom" (Kaison)

---

### 4. WORLD-FUSION ENVIRONMENTS (Dynamic Stages)

**Core Concept:**
Stages dynamically merge during combat, changing gravity, platforms, and hazards mid-fight.

**Example Fusions:**
- **Jungle + Tech-Ruins:** Trees grow through metal platforms, gravity shifts
- **Desert + Void-Citadel:** Sandstorm + static field, visibility decreases
- **Prime Pillar + Iron Canyons:** Verticality + horizontal space, multi-level combat

**Mechanics:**
- Players must use Core Resonance to "stabilize" ground they stand on
- Unstable areas = reduced stability, increased knockback
- Fusion events = cinematic transitions, environmental hazards

**Visual Design:**
- Split-screen effect during fusion
- Environment warping/merging animation
- Particle effects indicating stability zones

---

### 5. SILHOUETTE-DOMINANCE AI (Enemy Design)

**Every Boss Has a Unique "Silhouette Anchor":**
- Malakor: Glitches (flickering, stop-motion)
- Void King: Massive negative space (unmoving shadow)
- Resistance Bosses: Distinct geometric profiles

**Combat Philosophy:**
- Players must learn to fight the shape of the enemy, not just HP bar
- Silhouette = tells, telegraphs, and weaknesses
- Visual language > verbal instructions

## 🎨 VISUAL DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### AESTHETIC PRINCIPLES

**Matte-Mythic Visual Language:**
- **No Plastic:** Deep textures only (fur, stone, scarred metal)
- **No Neon:** Desaturated palette with selective color pops
- **Heavy Silhouettes:** Every character instantly readable in shadow
- **Heat Distortion:** High resonance = visible air warping around character

**Color Psychology:**
- **Amber/Gold:** Bovarr, memory, protection (warmth)
- **Red:** Jaxon, grit, power (intensity)
- **Blue:** Kaison, wisdom, precision (clarity)
- **White/Silver:** Silver, foresight, purity (detachment)
- **Purple/Midnight:** Lunara, fate, mystery (depth)
- **Static/Greyscale:** Void, silence, erasure (coldness)

**Lighting Philosophy:**
- **Hard Shadows:** High contrast, dramatic depth
- **Rim Lighting:** Characters pop against backgrounds
- **Core Glow:** Pulsing light from chest cores
- **Particle Density:** Minimal but impactful (quality over quantity)

---

### CHARACTER MODEL SPECIFICATIONS

**Kai-Jax:**
- Polycount: ~15,000 triangles (LOD0), ~8,000 (LOD1), ~4,000 (LOD2)
- Texture Resolution: 4K (diffuse, normal, roughness, emissive)
- Tail Physics: Dynamic cloth simulation (3 separate tails)
- Core Glow: Animated emissive texture (breathing effect)

**Silver:**
- Polycount: ~12,000 triangles (LOD0)
- Texture Resolution: 4K
- Quill-Blades: Physics-based floating animation
- Time Effects: Screen-space distortion shader

**Lunara:**
- Polycount: ~10,000 triangles (LOD0)
- Texture Resolution: 4K
- Constellation Patterns: Animated texture UV scrolling
- Lunar Rings: Transformable weapon system (bow/blade/shield)

## 🔊 AUDIO DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### MUSIC PHILOSOPHY

**Composition Style:**
- **Cinematic Orchestral:** Heavy drums, strings, brass
- **Electronic Elements:** Synthetic bass, glitch effects (Void themes)
- **Ethnic Fusion:** World music instruments (mythic Beastfolk culture)
- **Dynamic Layering:** Intensity increases with resonance

**Character Themes:**
- **Kai-Jax:** Dual melody (Jaxon's power chords + Kaison's precision synths), Bovarr's deep bass line
- **Silver:** Minimalist, time-bending (reversed samples, echo effects)
- **Lunara:** Ethereal, flowing (strings, chimes, lunar ambiance)

### SOUND DESIGN

**Core Resonance:**
- Low-frequency hum at base resonance
- Frequency increases with tier (audible pitch shift)
- At 100%: Thunderous bass drop (ascension sound)

**Impact Sounds:**
- **Light Hits:** Sharp, percussive (snap, crack)
- **Heavy Hits:** Deep, resonant (boom, thud)
- **Perfect Shield:** Crystalline chime (success sound)

**Voice Acting:**
- **Kai-Jax:** Dual-layer voice (Jaxon's growl + Kaison's calm overlay)
- **Bovarr:** Deep, gravelly (V.O. only, spectral echo effect)
- **Silver:** Calm, measured (minimal dialogue)
- **Lunara:** Soft but firm (oracle's authority)
