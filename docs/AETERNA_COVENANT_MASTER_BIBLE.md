# THE AETERNA COVENANT: MASTER STORY BIBLE
## Version 1.0 - The Iron-Will Edition

**Project Classification:** Mythic Platform Fighter / Narrative Epic  
**Aesthetic Core:** Matte-Mythic (High-fidelity, zero-saturation, Bronx-grit)  
**Target Audience:** The "Grown-Up Smash Fan"  
**World Law:** No Humans. Only Beastfolk & Cosmic-Forged Legends.

## 🌌 SECTION 1: THE COSMOLOGICAL ENGINE

### The Universal Law: Resonance vs. Silence

**THE WEAVE (The Noise):**
- The frequency of existence itself
- Every heartbeat, memory, and connection generates Weave energy
- Represented visually as vibrant color, dynamic movement, and heat distortion
- Protected by the Trinity and the Resistance

**THE SOURCE (The Silence):**
- The entropic void that seeks to delete data, feeling, and identity
- Represented as static, greyscale, and geometric dissolution
- Led by Malakor the Silencer and the Void King (Voidonus)
- The antithesis of all that resonates

**CORE RESONANCE:**
- Every living being in the Aeterna possesses a Core (Star, Chaos, or Memory engine)
- Power is measured in "Resonance Tiers" (Dormant → Harmonized → Ascended)
- Combat builds resonance through aggression, precision, and emotional connection
- Items are replaced by "Core Shards" that boost resonance or modify elemental output

### JAXON — THE VELOCITY FRACTURE (Solo Playable)

**Species:** Beastly Hedgehog-Lupine Hybrid  
**Design:** Young, lean, charcoal-furred hedgehog-lupine with long, electricity-flowing quills; feral but calculated appearance; smart and witty demeanor  
**Height:** 5'8" | Weight: 165 lbs (streamlined, speed-built)

**Origin:**
- Orphaned after village massacre, found by Bovarr with brother Kaison
- Trained by Bovarr in the Iron Canyons
- Represents raw speed, grit, and instinct

**Core Mechanics (Solo):**
- **Gravity:** g=9.8 (Standard speed physics)
- **Flicker-Strike (3f):** Frame-3 startup dash leaving Static-Decay after-images
- **Panic-Speed:** Passive reflex triggering 0.08s Hit-Stop on incoming projectiles for frame-perfect dodges
- **Electric Quills:** Long, flowing quills that generate blue-frequency lightning trails

**Visual Features:**
- Jagged electric quills that extend dynamically
- Blue-frequency lightning and Static Decay glitches trailing movement

**Arc:** From isolated speedster to learning brotherhood through Kaison

## 🎮 SECTION 5: GAMEPLAY LAWS (The Commandments)

### SILHOUETTE DOMINANCE
- Every character must be instantly readable in pure black-and-white
- If a fighter isn't recognizable in shadow, the design is rejected
- Cool first, functional second

### RESONANCE OVER ITEMS
- Power earned through combat momentum, not random spawns
- Core glow increases with resonance tier
- Items replaced by "Core Shards" (resonance boosters/modifiers)

### THE PATERNAL ANCHOR
- Bovarr's voice replaces background music at 100% resonance
- Provides "Low-Frequency Shield" (knockback resistance)
- Kai-Jax's 3rd Tail auto-blocks 25% projectile damage

### WORLD-FUSION ENVIRONMENTS
- Stages dynamically merge during combat
- Jungle + Tech-Ruins = changing gravity, platforms, hazards
- Players must use Core Resonance to stabilize ground
