🦁 B) SABERTOOTH MENTOR PRESENCE SYSTEM — BORAX

Rule:
Borax never replaces the player.
He changes reality, not outcomes.

Design Philosophy

Borax is not a helper NPC.
He is a pressure field.

Players should feel judged, not assisted.

⸻

HOW BORAX APPEARS (RARE, CONTROLLED)

Borax only manifests when:
	•	Player abuses power
	•	Player panics
	•	Player tries to force fusion
	•	Player survives something they shouldn’t

He never appears for:
	•	Basic fights
	•	Exploration
	•	Regular failures

⸻

BORAX EFFECTS (NO UI EXPLANATION)

When Borax is present:

Environmental Changes
	•	Wind pressure subtly shifts
	•	Dust moves unnaturally
	•	Sound compresses (low-frequency hum)
	•	Gravity feels heavier (micro input resistance)

Gameplay Changes
	•	Precision windows tighten
	•	Sloppy dodges fail harder
	•	Perfect play feels razor-sharp
	•	Enemies hesitate for half-beats

Borax never attacks for you—
but enemies know they are being measured.

⸻

BORAX INTERVENTION (EXTREMELY RARE)

Used only for:
	•	Boss gates
	•	Narrative hierarchy moments
	•	Preventing unearned power

When he intervenes:
	•	One strike
	•	One motion
	•	Fight ends

No loot.
No praise.
No dialogue.

Just law.

⸻

BORAX CANON LINE (LOCKED)

“Strength is nothing without restraint.
Restraint is nothing without memory.”
