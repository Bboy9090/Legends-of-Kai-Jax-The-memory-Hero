🧬 THE RULE OF THE GAME

Everything in the game is governed by four forces:
	1.	Hunger (Kar-Voth)
	2.	Law (Thryxen)
	3.	Sacrifice (Pyraxis)
	4.	Memory (Myrr’Kai)

Every system—combat, story, bosses, UI—answers to these forces.
